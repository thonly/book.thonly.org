# book.thonly.org

The library of books by Thon Ly, starting with *Two Singularities* (a new edition each 7 January from 2027).
Free to read; thanks welcomed; printed copies sold at cost; no advertising.

- **[`SPEC.md`](./SPEC.md)** — what is being built, and in what order. Read its header first: the doctrine it follows is
  kept elsewhere, and wins when the two disagree.
- **The record behind each edition** — the rules committed before each run, the transcripts and the ledger — is at
  [github.com/thonly/two-singularities](https://github.com/thonly/two-singularities).

Status: the library is live at the root (promoted 2026-10-15): five books received, beside the five given forward. The landing is in English and Khmer (`/km/`, our draft until a native reader approves it), written by [`scripts/build-site.py`](./scripts/build-site.py). *Two Singularities* is drawn and prototyped at [demo.thonly.org/book](https://demo.thonly.org/book/) (moved from `/demo` 2026-11-26; the old addresses redirect); its first edition is published here on 7 January 2027.
